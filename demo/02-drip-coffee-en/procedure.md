# How to Brew Drip Bag Coffee
## Get ready [prepare]
Prepare a mug, a drip coffee bag and a kettle. Boil the water and let it cool to about ninety degrees Celsius.
## Open the bag [open]
Hold the bag with both hands, tear off the top strip along the marked line. Open it gently to avoid spilling the coffee.
## Attach it to the mug [attach]
Spread the paper hooks, attach them to the rim of your mug. Check that the bag is secure before letting go.
## Let the coffee bloom [bloom]
To moisten the coffee evenly, pour a little hot water into the bag. Let it bloom for twenty seconds to release the aroma.
## Pour slowly [pour]
With the kettle ready, pour slowly in two or three rounds. Wait for the water to drain between pours so the bag does not overflow.
## Remove and enjoy [finish]
Once the water has drained, remove the bag from the mug. Watch out for hot drips! Your coffee is ready. Enjoy the aroma.
